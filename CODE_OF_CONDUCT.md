# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone taking part (in issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews) is expected
to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people. Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, trolling, or personal or
  political attacks.
- Do not publish anyone's private information without their explicit permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, GitHub user
[Yog-Sotho](https://github.com/Yog-Sotho), by a private channel: a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/Yog-Sotho/Homomorphic-Encryption-Service/security/advisories/new) works for reports that must stay
confidential. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled in confidence.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban
anyone who does, following the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
